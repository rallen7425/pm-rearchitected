# Rick Allen — Career Timeline

## UKG — Senior Director, Product Management, Payments & FinTech
**Jun 2024 – May 2026 · Lowell, MA (Hybrid)**

Led a team of product managers building next-generation pay and financial wellness products for frontline workers, serving roughly 13M end users across about 6,500 enterprise customers.

- Defined product vision and 0-to-1 strategy for a financial wellness portfolio for frontline workers, including an AI-powered financial health experience and strategic fintech partnerships — a standing start that grew into meaningful annual revenue.
- Led the next-generation launch of UKG Wallet (earned wage access, a UKG-branded Visa debit card, goal-based savings), growing transaction volume by an order of magnitude through new sales channels and a redesigned embedded experience that improved discovery and reduced friction.
- Led a competitive multi-vendor RFP process to select a strategic fintech partner, and developed a significant new distribution partnership (with Chime) to expand financial wellness reach.
- Spearheaded replatforming and modernization of the employee pay experience: redesigned web and mobile journeys on a new design system, launched a text-and-voice AI Pay Assistant, and unified the pay distribution platform across all user touchpoints with instant bank account verification (via an open-banking integration) to reduce payment errors and unlock fintech distribution partnerships.
- Drove adoption of an agentic AI-powered product discovery workflow across product and design (Claude Code, Replit), compressing research/prototyping/user-testing cycles from months to days — a process change he led for PMs and designers specifically (not the parallel, separately-driven engineering adoption of AI coding tools).
- Built and scaled the product organization from the ground up — recruiting and developing PMs, designers, and engineers into a team recognized for delivering outsized impact relative to its size. Operated as a hands-on player-coach: driving the most complex platform/partnership work directly while mentoring PMs across the broader org.
- Departed UKG in May 2026 (large-scale reduction/layoff).

## Earlier career

**Nuance Communications — Senior Product Manager, Mobile Applications (2007-2009/2010, Burlington, MA)**

**Sonera ZED — Product Manager (2000-2001, Boston, MA)**

**Arch Wireless — Product Manager (1997-2000, Westborough, MA)** — the paging company
Managed the launch of wireless messaging, mobile content, and e-commerce product offerings. His role evolved from managing the company website into something resembling modern product management: led a cross-functional team building the company's first e-commerce storefront (online pager sales, ahead of any competitor), which reduced customer acquisition cost by 60%, and navigated significant "channel conflict" with the legacy regional sales model while adding early internet-based features (email notifications, on-demand news/weather/sports) to pagers. Left after seeing an early Nokia mobile phone prototype and recognizing mobile — not pagers — was the future.

**Advertising / media planning (early-mid 1990s, Boston)** — started his career as a media planner at Boston-area ad agencies, analyzing audiences and negotiating with media sales reps across print, TV, radio, and outdoor. Left advertising after a client meeting with a product manager who impressed him with command of product, strategy, competitive landscape, and technical detail — the encounter that pulled him into product management.
