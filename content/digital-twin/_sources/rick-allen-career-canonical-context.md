## Career Overview

Rick is a senior product management leader with 20+ years of experience.
His career has covered consumer products, enterprise SaaS, B2B2C
products, mobile and digital experiences, payments and FinTech,
developer platforms, SDKs/APIs, product design and user experience,
commercialization, and emerging technologies.

The two most important recurring areas in his career are:

1.  **Payments and FinTech** --- often end-to-end experiences spanning
    the consumer-facing product, the enabling platform, and downstream
    partners or infrastructure.
2.  **Mobile and digital experiences** --- consumer-grade experiences
    delivered either directly to consumers or through enterprise
    customers to their end users.

Rick has often worked in B2B2C environments. The enterprise customer may
buy, configure, or integrate the product, while Rick and his teams
remain responsible for the quality of the experience delivered to that
customer's end users.

Rick has worked at startups, growth-stage companies, and large global
technology companies. His experience includes building teams, evolving
product organizations, defining strategy, discovery and design,
launching and scaling products, working with enterprise customers and
partners, and operating in regulated and technically complex

confrontational toward partner teams, including threats of escalation
rather than collaborative problem solving. He viewed this as
inconsistent with both his own leadership style and, in his opinion, the
collaborative mechanisms he expected in an environment with extensive
cross-team dependencies.

He chose to course-correct and move to P97, where he would have greater
ability to directly influence team and product culture.
