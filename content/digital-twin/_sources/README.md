# Digital Twin — source material

Raw, unedited documents the Digital Twin corpus is distilled from. **The chat does not read
anything in this folder.** `src/lib/digital-twin.ts` loads only the five named files one level
up (`bio.md`, `career-timeline.md`, `achievements.md`, `philosophy.md`, `faq.md`), so files here
are archive/reference only and never enter the system prompt.

Workflow: new source material lands here verbatim, then the genuinely new, non-sensitive facts
get hand-merged into the five corpus files (and the identical masters in
`PMRearchitected/Rick's Digital Twin/Corpus-Ready/`).

Deliberately kept out of the corpus:
- Compensation figures and exact revenue multipliers off a low base (corpus generalizes these).
- Names of recruiters, hiring managers, or company contacts.
- Company-specific framing for live/sensitive hiring conversations.
